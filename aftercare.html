Aftercare

Support your body after your session with simple practices designed to encourage recovery, relaxation, and lasting wellness support.

Immediately After Your Session

Allow your body time to adjust after your massage. Drink water, move gently, and pay attention to how your body feels.

Hydration & Rest

Hydration, rest, and gentle self-care can help support your body's natural recovery process after therapeutic massage.

Gentle Movement

Light stretching, walking, and comfortable movement may help maintain mobility and reduce feelings of stiffness between sessions.

Listen to Your Body

Everyone responds differently to massage. Notice how your body feels and communicate any questions or concerns before your next appointment.

Continue Your Wellness Journey

Consistent care can help support relaxation, mobility, and your personal wellness goals through a personalized approach.

After Your First Session

It is normal to notice changes in how your body feels after massage. Some people experience immediate relaxation, while others notice gradual changes over time. Your therapist may recommend personalized self-care strategies based on your needs and goals.
