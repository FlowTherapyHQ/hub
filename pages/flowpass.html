A Little Flexibility

How Rollover Works

One unused monthly session may roll over into the immediately following month. This allows you to hold no more than two available sessions at one time: your current monthly session and one rollover session.

An unused rollover session does not continue accumulating beyond that following month.

Example

If you do not use your July session, it may be used during August. Your August membership session will also remain available during August.

The unused July session will not roll into September.

Helpful Information

FlowPass FAQ

What is included in my monthly membership?

Your membership includes one Full Body Flow massage each month at the duration selected when you enroll, plus the member benefits described on this page.

Can I transfer my session to someone else?

No. FlowPass memberships and monthly membership sessions are nontransferable.

What happens if I cannot use my session?

One unused session may roll into the immediately following month. Unlimited session accumulation is not permitted.

Can members purchase additional massages?

Yes. Members may purchase additional Full Body Flow sessions matching their membership duration and receive $25 off the standard rate.

Which FlowBoosts are available for $15?

Eligible FlowBoosts receive member pricing of $15. Crown Flow and Time Boost are excluded from this benefit.

How do I cancel my membership?

After completing the initial three-month commitment, the membership continues month-to-month and requires 30 days’ advance cancellation notice.
