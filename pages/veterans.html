Every session is guided by your preferences, comfort level, wellness goals, and informed consent.

Your First Appointment

What to Expect

Knowing what will happen before and during an appointment can make the experience feel more comfortable and predictable.

1

Complete Your Intake

Your confidential intake form provides information about your health history, areas of concern, goals, medications, injuries, and massage preferences.

2

Discuss Your Goals

Before the session, you and your therapist will review your needs, identify areas of focus, discuss pressure preferences, and answer any questions.

3

Receive Personalized Care

Your session will be adapted to your comfort and may include therapeutic massage, focused bodywork, stretching, or other appropriate techniques.

4

Continue Your Wellness Journey

After your appointment, you may receive personalized aftercare, educational resources, and recommendations to support your goals between sessions.

Comfort, Consent & Communication

You Remain in Control

FlowTherapy recognizes that every client has different preferences, experiences, and comfort needs. Communication is encouraged before, during, and after every session.

Your comfort, privacy, and informed consent remain the foundation of every session. We encourage open communication so your treatment can be adjusted to meet your needs at any time.

  • You choose the areas addressed during the session.
  • You may remain clothed when appropriate for the service.
  • Draping and privacy are maintained during table massage.
  • You may request quiet or conversation.
  • You may ask for changes to pressure, positioning, or technique.
  • You may pause or end the session at any time.

Support Beyond the Appointment

FlowSummary

FlowSummary is a client-friendly recap designed to help you understand the focus of your session and continue supporting your wellness goals after the appointment.

FlowSummary is separate from your confidential clinical documentation and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a healthcare provider.

Your FlowSummary May Include

  • Today’s areas of focus
  • Techniques or approaches used
  • What you may notice after the session
  • Personalized aftercare guidance
  • FlowHomework for continued support
  • Ideas to discuss at your next appointment

Between-Session Support

FlowHomework

FlowHomework provides simple, realistic actions that may help you stay connected to your wellness goals between appointments.

Movement

Gentle movement, stretching, mobility exercises, or a FlowResource selected for your needs.

Recovery

Suggestions such as rest, hydration, heat, cold, or activity modification when appropriate.

Awareness

Notice patterns in tension, movement, daily activities, stress, or symptoms to discuss at your next visit.

Education

Relevant FlowNotes and downloadable FlowResources that support your understanding between appointments.

Helpful Information

Veterans Resource Center FAQ

Is FlowTherapy currently accepting VA referrals?

Not yet. FlowTherapy is currently pursuing credentialing and enrollment and is not presently accepting VA Community Care authorizations or billing the VA.

Can Veterans still book appointments privately?

Yes. Veterans may book available FlowTherapy services as self-pay clients at the standard published rate.

Do I have to discuss my military experiences?

No. You decide what information you are comfortable sharing. Your therapist only needs information relevant to providing safe, appropriate massage therapy.

Can I request a quiet session?

Yes. You may request quiet, limited conversation, or communication only when needed for comfort and safety.

Can I remain clothed?

Some services and techniques may be provided through comfortable clothing. Options can be discussed before your appointment.

Can massage replace medical care?

No. Massage therapy does not replace medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment. Please continue working with your healthcare providers and follow their recommendations.
