Aftercare

Support your body after your session with simple practices designed to encourage recovery, relaxation, and lasting wellness support.

Immediately After Your Session

Give your body time to adjust after your massage. Drink water as you normally would, move gently, and allow yourself time to rest if needed. Pay attention to how your body feels over the next 24 to 48 hours.

Hydration & Rest

Stay hydrated, prioritize quality sleep, and give yourself permission to slow down if your body feels like it needs extra rest. Gentle self-care can help you continue feeling your best after your session.

Gentle Movement

Light stretching, walking, and comfortable movement may help maintain mobility and reduce feelings of stiffness between sessions.

Listen to Your Body

Everyone responds differently to massage. Pay attention to how your body feels in the hours and days after your session. If you have questions, unexpected discomfort, or concerns, please reach out before your next appointment.

Continue Your Wellness Journey

Consistent care can help support relaxation, mobility, and your personal wellness goals through a personalized approach.

After Your First Session

It is normal to notice changes in how your body feels after massage. Some people experience immediate relaxation, while others notice gradual changes over time. Your therapist may recommend personalized self-care strategies based on your individual needs and wellness goals.
