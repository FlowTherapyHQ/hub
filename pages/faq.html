Getting ready

Before Your Appointment

What should I wear?

Wear whatever makes you comfortable. Most clients arrive in everyday clothing and change or undress only to their personal comfort level before the session.

The most important thing is that you feel at ease and can move comfortably before and after your appointment.

Do I have to undress?

No. You may undress only to your personal comfort level. Some clients remain partially or fully clothed depending on the service, their preferences, and the areas being addressed.

Proper draping is always used to protect your privacy, and only the area receiving massage is uncovered.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Complete any required forms before your visit when possible, arrive with enough time to settle in, and share any injuries, medical conditions, medications, allergies, or areas of concern.

You may also note your goals for the session, preferred pressure, areas you want addressed, and any areas you would like avoided.

Do you work with prenatal clients?

Yes. Prenatal massage is available with positioning, bolstering, and techniques adapted for comfort throughout pregnancy.

Please share pregnancy-related concerns, complications, recent changes, or healthcare-provider restrictions before your session. Medical permission may be requested when appropriate.

Comfort and recovery

During & After Your Session

Will the massage hurt?

Therapeutic massage should not feel overwhelming or unsafe. Some techniques may create temporary intensity or tenderness, but you should still feel able to breathe, relax, and communicate.

Pressure can be adjusted at any time. More pressure is not automatically more effective, and you never need to endure discomfort to receive a meaningful session.

How often should I get a massage?

Frequency depends on your goals, lifestyle, activity level, budget, health considerations, and how your body responds.

Some clients schedule occasionally for relaxation, while others benefit from more consistent care during periods of increased stress, physical demand, or recovery. Your therapist may offer guidance without pressuring you into a schedule.

Should I drink water afterward?

Continue drinking water according to your normal needs and thirst. Massage does not require excessive hydration.

Eating normally, choosing gentle movement, resting when needed, and paying attention to how your body feels can support a comfortable recovery.

Is soreness after massage normal?

Mild, temporary tenderness may occur after focused or deeper work, especially in areas that were already sensitive, overworked, or tense.

Soreness should generally improve rather than progressively worsen. Sharp, severe, persistent, or concerning pain should not be dismissed as normal aftercare.

Appointments and policies

Scheduling & Policies

What if I am running late?

Please let FlowTherapy know as soon as possible. Every reasonable effort will be made to accommodate you, but the appointment may need to end at the originally scheduled time to avoid affecting the next client.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

As a small practice, your appointment time is reserved just for you. Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Early notice makes it possible to offer that time to another client.

What forms do I need to complete?

New clients should complete the FlowStart Intake before their first appointment. Additional forms, such as the Detailed Body Map or Physician Permission Form, may be optional or requested only in certain situations.

Current forms and practice documents are available on the Client Forms page.

Can I communicate preferences during the session?

Absolutely. You are encouraged to speak up about pressure, positioning, temperature, draping, music, conversation, sensitive areas, or anything else affecting your comfort.

Your preferences can change during the session, and you may request an adjustment or ask to stop at any time.

Your comfort comes first

Massage is collaborative. You do not need to tolerate uncomfortable pressure, unwanted conversation, a difficult position, or any technique that does not feel right. Communication is always welcome, and consent can be changed or withdrawn at any time.

  • Ask for lighter or firmer pressure
  • Request positioning changes
  • Identify areas to avoid
  • Choose conversation or quiet
  • Pause or end the session

Still have a question?

Every client and situation is different. Reach out before your appointment when you need help choosing a service, completing forms, discussing accessibility, or preparing for your visit.
