Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your appointment. If you don't see your question here, feel free to reach out.

What should I wear?

Wear whatever makes you comfortable. Most clients arrive in everyday clothing and undress only to their comfort level before the session. Proper draping is always used to protect your privacy.

Do I have to undress?

Massage should remain within your comfort level. Some techniques may feel intense or tender, but you are always encouraged to communicate so the pressure, positioning, or technique can be adjusted at any time.

Will the massage hurt?

Therapeutic massage should never feel overwhelming. Some techniques may create temporary discomfort, but communication is encouraged throughout your session so pressure can be adjusted at any time.

How often should I get a massage?

Every person is different. Your therapist may recommend a schedule based on your goals, lifestyle, activity level, and how your body responds to treatment.

What if I'm running late?

Please let us know as soon as possible. Every effort will be made to accommodate you, although your session may need to be shortened to avoid impacting other scheduled appointments.

What if I need to cancel?

Please provide as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This allows the opportunity to offer that appointment time to another client.

Do you work with prenatal clients?

Yes. Prenatal massage is available with positioning and techniques adapted for comfort throughout pregnancy. Please share any pregnancy-related concerns or restrictions, and consult your healthcare provider when medically advised.

Should I drink water afterward?

Staying hydrated after your massage can support your overall well-being. Continue normal hydration and pay attention to how your body feels over the next day or two.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Complete any required forms before your visit when possible, arrive a few minutes early, and let your therapist know about any injuries, medical conditions, or areas of concern.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Please reach out before your appointment if you have any questions about services, scheduling, or what to expect during your visit.
